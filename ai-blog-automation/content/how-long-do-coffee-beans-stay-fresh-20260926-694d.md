The honest answer to how long do coffee beans stay fresh is: trust your palate, not the package. If the grounds still bloom with a lively aroma and your cup tastes bright and complex, the beans are in their prime. When the scent turns flat or musty, it's time to replace them — even if the roast date says otherwise.

Whole beans stored in an airtight container, kept away from heat and light, hold their character far longer than pre-ground coffee. The [National Coffee Association](https://www.ncausa.org/) confirms that proper storage is the single biggest factor in preserving freshness, so grind only what you need.
